Subject: [RFC PATCH] introduce scheme re-ordering to reduce age.min sensitivity

Problem Statement
=================

Tuning DAMOS (DAMON-based Operation Schemes) parameters—specifically
age.min for operations like pageout can be challenging. Setting a "good"
age.min that performs well universally across different workloads and
hardware devices is difficult because memory access patterns and
pressure vary widely.

Currently, if a scheme's age.min is configured too aggressively, it may
cause premature reclamation. If configured too leniently, it may fail to
meet target objectives effectively.
